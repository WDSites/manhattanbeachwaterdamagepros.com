Manhattan Beach, the home area

Manhattan Beach itself uses ZIP 90266, and ZIP 90254 also covers part of the city. The city is a retail water supplier within the West Basin Municipal Water District, and its Community Development Department at 1400 Highland Avenue handles permits.

Inside the city, the Sand Section, Hill Section, Tree Section, Manhattan Village, Liberty Village and El Porto are all covered by the same dispatch line. Water damage here follows the same stages described on the services page.

Why each nearby town gets its own page

A burst pipe in Hermosa Beach and a backed-up drain in Hawthorne are different problems with different local details. Hermosa Beach has a high water table the city itself mentions, El Segundo runs its own water and sewer system, and Hawthorne changed water providers in September 2026.

Those differences change who you call about a main-line break, which permit office you ask about a rebuild and which ZIP codes to quote when you phone dispatch. Reading your town page first can save a few minutes on the call.

What to have ready when you call from a nearby town

Give the street address, the ZIP code and the nearest major road. For Hermosa Beach and Redondo Beach that is usually Pacific Coast Highway, for El Segundo it is Sepulveda Boulevard, and for Hawthorne and Lawndale it is Hawthorne Boulevard or Aviation Boulevard.

Mention whether the water is clean, gray or from a drain. That one detail changes which crew and equipment are sent, and it is the first thing the dispatcher asks about.

How response works across the area

The 24/7 dispatch line is the same for every town, and crews are sent as availability and traffic allow. We do not promise an arrival time, because the roads between these towns can back up without warning. We are plain about timing: a dispatcher gives you an honest read on what to expect for your address.

While you wait, the steps on each town page apply everywhere: close the main water valve, keep clear of wet electrical equipment, lift valuables and photograph the damage before you move anything.

If your town is not listed

The list covers the towns we serve most. If you live somewhere else in the South Bay, call and give the address, since the dispatcher can tell you whether a crew can reach it. We would rather answer that question honestly than promise service we cannot deliver.

Common questions

Is there a travel charge for nearby towns?

Scope and price are explained before work starts, and distance is one of the details the dispatcher covers on the call. Ask when you phone and the answer will be specific to your address.

Do all towns get the same 24/7 dispatch?

Yes. Nobody hangs up on a 2 a.m. call here. A dispatcher answers, asks what is wet and where the water is coming from, and tells you what to do first.

Which ZIP code should I give when I call?

Give the ZIP on your mailing address. The town pages list the ZIP codes tied to each place, and one ZIP, 90260, is shared by Lawndale and part of Hawthorne.

Can I check who supplies my water?

Your water bill names the supplier. Several of these towns have more than one supplier, so the bill is the quickest way to know whom to call about a main-line problem.
