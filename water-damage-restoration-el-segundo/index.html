When water is spreading in El Segundo

Keep clear of live electricity. Turn off breakers only if the panel is dry and you can walk to it without crossing water, and ring 911 or the utility about any live-wire or gas worry before calling us.

If water is still spreading in El Segundo

These steps buy time. Stop at any that look unsafe.

Shut off supply

Close the main valve or the fixture shutoff. A reading of your meter that keeps moving afterward suggests a leak elsewhere.

Stop the drains

For a sewage backup, stop all water use in the home and keep everyone out of the affected rooms.

Skip wet outlets

Never plug or unplug anything standing in water. Call 911 or the utility for sparks, buzzing or a gas smell.

Raise and remove

Lift valuables, papers and electronics to higher surfaces, and pull rugs into the sun.

Call with details

Tell the dispatcher the address, the source, and whether anything is dripping into a floor below.

Common questions about El Segundo

Do you cover every ZIP code in El Segundo?

El Segundo is served under ZIP code 90245, which spans the city from the beachfront edge near Dockweiler State Beach through Old Town and out toward Sepulveda Boulevard. If you live in Playa del Rey or Westchester, you are in Los Angeles, not El Segundo, though you can still call the same line.

Is emergency water removal available in El Segundo?

Yes. The line answers 24 hours a day, and emergencies get first priority for pump-out. Crews go out the same day whenever workload permits, and nothing is promised on timing until the details are known.

Is a sewer backup my responsibility or the city's?

It depends on where the blockage sits. A problem in the city's main is a Public Works matter, while a blockage on your own line is yours. Either way, standing sewage water needs professional removal.

Do I need a permit for repairs after water damage?

Sometimes. It depends on the work, so ask the city's Building and Safety Division about walls, plumbing or electrical changes. Extraction and drying are usually done before any rebuild begins.

Can you work in an office or shop here?

Yes, and in El Segundo that includes small commercial spaces in Old Town and Smoky Hollow. Tell the dispatcher it is a commercial space and whether other tenants are affected.
