Mitigation for tight lots and shared walls

The first days decide whether damage stays small. Water mitigation covers shutting the source, removing water, mapping moisture and setting air movers and LGR dehumidifiers. In a compact home, equipment placement matters, because air needs a path to move through and over wet surfaces.

We check readings daily and adjust fans as areas dry. When two units share a wall, we usually dry both sides so moisture does not hide between them.

Pumping out standing water

When there is depth on the floor, emergency water removal comes before anything else. Pumps and extraction units remove water from carpet, pad and hard floors, then moisture meters and thermal cameras show where it traveled. Wet insulation and low drywall are cut out when they will not dry in place.
