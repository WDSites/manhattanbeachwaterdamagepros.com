Water category decides the work

The water's source sets how aggressively materials are removed, as described in the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration.

CategoryTypical sourceWhat it means for materials
Category 1Broken supply line, overflowing sinkOften dried in place if caught within a day or so
Category 2Washer or dishwasher discharge, toilet overflow without solidsPorous materials are usually removed, hard surfaces cleaned
Category 3Sewage backup, rising floodwaterContaminated, so porous materials are removed and areas sanitized

A category can worsen with time, so water that begins clean may need stricter handling after two or three days.

Mold risk when drying is late

If water is still spreading in Redondo Beach

Start with whichever of these you can do safely, then keep your phone line open for the dispatcher.

Find your shutoff

Check the unit shutoff beneath the sink or toilet, then the main valve at the meter or the front hose bib line.

Warn the floor below

In a condo or apartment, knock on the neighbor's door before water drops through the ceiling.

Power only if dry

Leave breakers alone if you would have to cross wet floor to reach the panel. Call 911 or the utility instead.

Save and snap

Rescue photos and documents, then photograph the damage and the source before anything is thrown away.

Call and describe

Give the address, the source, how long it ran, and whether the water looks clean or dirty.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Redondo Beach?

Is emergency water removal available in Redondo Beach?

Yes, at any hour. Phone the line, explain what the water looks like, and the dispatcher confirms what is possible today. Arrival times are not promised in advance, since they depend on crew availability and the type of loss.

Does a condo leak above me change what you do?

It does. We document where the water entered, check the ceiling and walls with a moisture meter, and dry your unit while the source above is stopped. Your association or the unit owner may need to be contacted.

How do I know whether Cal Water or Golden State Water serves my home?
